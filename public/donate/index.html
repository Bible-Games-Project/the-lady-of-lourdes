How we count

Players reached

Platforms report different things: downloads (every time a game is fetched, including re-downloads), installations (one per device), active users (people playing in a given month), activated copies (Steam keys) and players (accounts that actually launched the game). To count people reached, we use the figure closest to “one person, one game” on each platform:

  • Google Play — devices that installed the game for the first time. Updates and re-installs on the same device are excluded. Google no longer reports first installs per account, so someone with a phone and a tablet counts twice.
  • App Store — first-time downloads. Apple counts these once per Apple ID; re-downloads and updates are excluded.
  • Steam — lifetime unique users from Steamworks: Steam accounts that actually played the game.

Google, Apple and Valve never tell us who their players are, so we cannot recognise the same person on two platforms or in two of our games. We add the platforms together and say so here, rather than claim to count unique people. Monthly active users are not added, because they measure who is playing now, not how many people we have reached.

Supporters & purchases

Every PayPal donor counts once, identified by their PayPal account, whether they gave once, give every month, or both. Donors whose gifts were all refunded are not counted. To this we add every paid purchase of our games on Google Play, the App Store and Steam, minus refunds. Free downloads are not purchases. The stores do not tell us who their buyers are, so a buyer who also donates is counted once as a supporter and once as a purchase.

Total contributions

The money the project actually receives, in euros. PayPal donations and monthly support are counted after PayPal's fees, refunds and chargebacks. Store sales are counted as the developer's share after Google's, Apple's or Valve's commission, taxes withheld by the store and refunds. Every transaction is counted once, by its transaction or order ID. Amounts in other currencies are converted with the European Central Bank reference rate of the day of the transaction or report.

Updates

The figures come from the official reports of PayPal, Google Play Console, App Store Connect and Steamworks, refreshed once a day. Store earnings reports are monthly, so the latest month's sales may appear a few weeks later. If a platform cannot be reached, we keep its last verified figure and mark it as possibly out of date. We never estimate or round up.

Who we are

Two Brothers, One Mission

We are Pau and Joan Sabé, two brothers who started Bible Games Project with a simple wish: to make the Bible and the Christian faith more accessible through games that are fun to play and meaningful to remember.

We make our games for children, families and people around the world. We believe a game can be a beautiful way to walk alongside the people of the Bible, understand their faith and carry its message into everyday life.

— Pau & Joan
