Our mission

Games That Bring the Bible to Life

Bible Games Project was founded by brothers Pau and Joan Sabé with one clear mission: to create entertaining and meaningful Christian video games for children, families and people around the world.

We believe games can be a beautiful way to discover the stories of the Bible — to walk alongside its people, understand their faith and carry its message into everyday life.

— Pau & Joan
